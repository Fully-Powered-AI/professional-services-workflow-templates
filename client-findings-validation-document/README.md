# Client findings validation document

Before research becomes recommendations, the engagement lead sends the client one document: each material finding beside its evidence, the implication proposed and a clear request to confirm, correct or defer it. Nothing is added that the evidence does not show.

*Workflow template · Professional services · Project and delivery · version 0.1.0 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflow templates/client-findings-validation-document/)*

## The outcome

The client sees what the team found, what it rests on and what the team proposes to do about it, in one place and in a fixed shape. Each finding ends in a plain request: confirm it, correct it, or defer it. Evidence that points the other way sits beside the finding, and anything the team has assumed and not yet shown is written down under it.

The recommendations are then built on findings the client has actually answered. A finding the client defers is not counted as agreed, and the work that depends on it waits. The engagement lead checks every item of evidence against its source, decides what goes to the client, and decides what to do with each answer.

## Who starts the work, and when

The engagement lead, or a consultant working for them, starts it when a workstream has finished its research and interviews and is about to turn them into recommendations. It works best a few days before the findings are presented, while there is still time to chase a missing source or reword a claim the evidence cannot carry.

The client sponsor, or whoever the decision brief names as the person who decides, receives the document once the engagement lead has approved it.

## What they supply

- **The decision brief:** the engagement, the decisions the client has to make, and who decides each.
- **The evidence register:** one row for each interview, data extract, document or observation, with its date, who it came from, what it says, its limits and whether it may be shown to the client.
- **The candidate findings:** one row for each, with the evidence behind it, any evidence against it, what is assumed, the implication the team proposes and whether it is material.
- **Today's date and the date the client should reply by.**
- **Optional settings:** which words count as strong quantity words, how many interviews must stand behind them, and the wording of the three replies. Defaults are built in.
- **For the second run only:** the client's written replies, by finding, with who gave them and when, together with the same decision brief, register and findings again.

The workflow template reads only what is supplied. It does not search, contact anyone or open a system.

## What comes out

One document in three parts. The first part is for the client: each material finding with its evidence, its counter-evidence, its open assumptions, the implication proposed and a response request with three boxes. The second part is for the engagement lead only, to remove before anything is sent: findings held back and why, wording the evidence does not carry, withheld items, figures that were checked, and a numbered list of decisions. The third part is written after the client replies, and records each answer in the client's own words.

## What has to change for your company

Decide who counts as the client's decider for each kind of finding. Set what makes a finding material in your engagements, and whether findings from workshops count as evidence. Choose how many interviews you want behind a claim that uses a word such as most or usually. Fix the wording of the three responses to match how your clients reply, and the number of days they have. Agree what you will not show a client, such as an interviewee who asked not to be passed on, and how that is marked in the register.
